Flash the Ai-Thinker ESP32 Audio Kit (first time)
=================================================

Connect the board's UART port (micro-USB, marked "UART"), and run:

  esptool.py --chip esp32 -p /dev/ttyUSB0 -b 460800 write_flash 0x0 leadin-audiokit-full.bin

If the board does not answer, hold the BOOT key while esptool connects.
Or, from the source folder:  make flash

Then prepare the SD card:

  1. Format the card as FAT32.
  2. Put LEADIN.CFG in the root of the card, with two lines:
       SSID=YourNetwork
       PASSWORD=YourPassword
  3. Copy your .iso files, or .cue files with their .bin files.
  4. Set the DIP switches for CMD and DATA3 to ON.

The web UI is at http://leadin.local/.

Later updates go through the web UI: open "System", turn on "Firmware
update", and choose the leadin.bin from this folder. The switch is off
again after every restart.

See README.md for the first test on the board.
